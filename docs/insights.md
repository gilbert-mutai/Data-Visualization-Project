# Key Insights

Share of renewable energy in gross final energy consumption, Eurostat `nrg_ind_ren`, 2016 to 2025 (2025 provisional, 30 countries). View in italics.

## Q1: Leaders and Laggards

- **Nordic countries lead:** Norway 82.4%, Sweden 65.4%, Finland 53.0%, Denmark 48.2%, Latvia 46.3% (2025). *Overview*
- **Lowest:** Belgium 14.9%, Slovakia 16.3%, Ireland 17.2%, Poland 18.7%, Malta 18.8%. *Overview*
- **Few countries meet a target:** 10 of 30 are above 32% (the original 2030 EU target), only 6 above 42.5% (the revised 2030 target). *Overview with the target slider*

## Q2: Trends

- **The EU-27 rose from 18.0% to 26.2%** (2016 → 2025), steadily except for a flat 2021 after the 2020 jump. *Trends with EU-27 line*
- **Our focus countries in 2025:** Denmark 48.2%, Austria 44.2%, Spain 26.0%, Germany 23.9%, France 23.9%, Hungary 19.6%, Poland 18.7%. Hungary is 6.6 points below the EU-27. *Trends*

## Q3: Movers

- **Largest gains since 2016:** Netherlands +16.9 pp, Denmark +16.5, Luxembourg +14.8, Finland +14.1, Norway +13.2. *Movers*
- **Median gain: +8.7 pp.** Only Moldova (−4.8) and Montenegro (−1.7) declined. *Movers*
- **Single years can swing:** Bosnia and Herzegovina +12.7 pp in 2018, Denmark −5.3 pp in 2020. *Matrix, year-on-year view*

## Q4: Spread

- **The median country stays near 21% to 25%,** while the middle half of countries spans about 16 to 22 points: the gap between leaders and laggards is not closing. *Spread & Outliers*
- **Norway and Iceland are the only outliers** (1.5 × IQR rule), every year; Iceland has no 2025 value yet. *Spread & Outliers*
